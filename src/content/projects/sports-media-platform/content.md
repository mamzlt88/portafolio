# Intro

A leading sports media platform focused on high school football content, rankings, and live coverage needed to evolve its digital experience to support growth.

As the business expanded into streaming, subscriptions, and digital engagement, its platform required a more scalable and unified ecosystem.

This project was part of a broader transformation initiative aimed at integrating content, streaming, customer engagement, and data into a cohesive platform.

Within an 8-week discovery and design phase, the work focused on defining the **user experience, visual direction, and design foundation** required to support this transformation.

**Outcome:** Defined UX direction across a multi-platform ecosystem · Delivered a scalable UI foundation with new branding · Established proto-personas, user flows, and IA · Aligned stakeholders across product, design, and engineering

# Discovery

The platform ecosystem included multiple interconnected systems:

- Content and editorial workflows (CMS)
- Customer and sponsor management (CRM)
- Streaming and video infrastructure
- Analytics and business intelligence

Each system had evolved independently, resulting in a fragmented experience for both users and internal teams. The discovery phase focused on understanding how these systems interacted and how users moved across them.

# Core Challenge

The challenge was not simply improving an interface — it was **redefining an entire digital ecosystem**.

Users experienced this as:

- Disconnected journeys between content, live games, and subscriptions
- Confusion around navigation and platform structure
- Inconsistent branding and interaction patterns

From a business and system perspective:

- No unified architecture to support growth
- Data and insights fragmented across systems
- Monetization opportunities limited by disconnected experiences

This created the need to move toward a **fully integrated, scalable platform**.

# Strategy

The approach focused on building **clarity before scale**. Instead of designing isolated features, the work aligned three key layers:

**Experience Design:** Define user flows, personas, and interaction patterns

**Design System:** Create a UI Kit and visual direction to ensure consistency and scalability

**System Alignment:** Ensure UX aligns with CMS, CRM, streaming, and analytics systems

This ensured that design decisions supported both **user experience and business goals**.

# Research

Research focused on understanding both the audience and internal stakeholders.

**Proto-Personas** represented key users: fans consuming content and live games, subscribers accessing premium features, and internal teams managing content and operations.

Key activities included proto-persona development, user flow mapping, information architecture, and stakeholder alignment. These personas guided design decisions and prioritization.

# Design Foundation

A UI audit revealed inconsistencies: fragmented visual identity, lack of hierarchy and clarity, inconsistent interaction patterns.

Branding discovery explored how the platform could better reflect the identity and emotional connection of sports culture. This resulted in a **Stylescape** that defined the look and feel of the new platform.

The foundation included a reusable **UI Kit** with components, layout and interaction rules, and scalable structure. UX artifacts included proto-personas, user flows, and information architecture.

# Outcomes

Usability testing validated navigation clarity, content discovery, and subscription and streaming flows. Feedback was used to refine designs and improve usability.

| Focus Area | Before | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **User Experience** | Fragmented journeys | Unified content, streaming, and subscription flows |
| **Branding** | Inconsistent identity | Clear and cohesive visual direction |
| **UX Structure** | Undefined flows | Structured IA and user journeys |
| **System Alignment** | Disconnected tools | Integrated ecosystem approach |
| **Team Alignment** | Siloed work | Shared direction and roadmap |

# Reflections

This project highlighted the complexity of designing for a content-driven platform evolving into a full digital ecosystem. Balancing editorial needs, streaming capabilities, and monetization required aligning multiple perspectives into one coherent experience.

**Lessons:** Media platforms require both content and system thinking. Branding plays a key role in engagement. Early alignment reduces complexity later. Design systems enable consistency at scale. UX must connect content, technology, and business.
