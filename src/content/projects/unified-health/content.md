# Intro

UnifiedHealth Solutions experienced rapid growth through multiple acquisitions. While this expanded their market presence, it also created fragmented processes, duplicated work, and inconsistent service delivery across products.

The company initiated a discovery phase to redesign and unify these processes while aligning them with Salesforce Service Cloud.

The work focused on understanding the current state, defining a scalable process model, and translating it into system flows that could support efficiency and growth.

**Outcome:** Defined a unified process across multiple products · Reduced duplication and operational inefficiencies · Created a scalable foundation aligned with Salesforce · Identified automation opportunities to improve speed and consistency

# Discovery

The organization operated through multiple acquired companies, each with its own workflows, tools, and service models.

Although the services were similar, the way they were delivered varied significantly. Teams worked in silos, and processes were not aligned across products or systems.

This created a complex operational landscape where visibility was limited and collaboration was difficult. The discovery phase focused on understanding how these different parts worked together and where the main friction points existed.

# Core Challenge

The main issue was **process fragmentation**. Because each company had developed independently:

- Processes were inconsistent across products
- Systems were not aligned
- Teams operated with different workflows and rules

This led to inefficiencies and duplicated work, delays in service delivery, lack of visibility across operations, and inconsistent user and client experiences.

The challenge was to create a **unified process** that could work across all products while remaining flexible enough to support future growth.

# Strategy

The approach focused on two key directions:

**Process Unification:** Understand and map all existing processes, identify common patterns and differences, define a standardized, scalable process model.

**Platform Alignment:** Use Salesforce Service Cloud as the foundation, adapt processes to fit Salesforce objects and workflows, leverage platform capabilities to improve efficiency.

Additional focus areas included supporting revenue-generating activities, identifying automation opportunities, and reducing manual work.

# Research

Research was conducted through interviews with stakeholders and users involved in the processes. This helped identify pain points, variations between products, and user responsibilities across each stage.

Key insight: The problem was not a lack of processes, but a lack of **alignment and structure** across them.

Key questions guided the work: Where are the bottlenecks? Who are the users at each stage? Where does revenue happen? What can be automated? How can processes align with Salesforce?

# System Design

To ensure the process aligned with the system, **OOUX (ORCA analysis)** was used. This helped define key objects, relationships between them, and actions users perform.

The processes were then mapped into Salesforce Service Cloud — cases, workflows, queues, and interactions. This ensured that the solution was both conceptually clear and technically implementable.

Wireframes were created to represent the unified process visually, ensure usability and clarity, and validate ideas with stakeholders.

# Outcomes

The redesigned process introduced clear stages, defined user roles, reduced manual tasks, better team alignment, and automation opportunities through Salesforce.

| Focus Area | Before | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Process Structure** | Fragmented across companies | Unified and standardized model |
| **Efficiency** | Manual and duplicated work | Streamlined workflows |
| **Visibility** | Limited cross-team visibility | Clear process tracking |
| **Consistency** | Different approaches per product | Aligned service delivery |
| **Scalability** | Difficult to expand | Foundation for growth |

# Reflections

This project highlighted the complexity of working across multiple systems and teams. The biggest challenge was not defining a better process, but aligning stakeholders around a shared model. Balancing business goals, user needs, and Salesforce constraints required continuous collaboration and iteration.

**Lessons:** Process clarity is essential for scalability. Alignment across teams is as important as system design. Mapping before designing reduces complexity. Systems like Salesforce require adaptation, not just implementation.
